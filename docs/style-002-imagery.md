## Shared prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create one portrait 2:3 full-body editorial fashion photograph for the On the Go college white T-shirt styling website. [Outfit specification below.] The clothes are gender-neutral outfit inspiration on an adult model in their twenties. Entire person and shoes visible with comfortable margins, natural confident relaxed pose, realistic anatomy and fabric, soft natural daylight, warm beige stone/concrete urban backdrop, restrained sophisticated editorial photography matching a warm paper and dark-green website. One model, one outfit, plain unprinted opaque WHITE T-shirt with NO graphic, brand or logo. No text, labels, collage or watermark. Preserve realistic proportions and practical achievable everyday styling. Output a project asset and save a local file.
