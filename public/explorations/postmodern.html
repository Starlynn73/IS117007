Why this direction?

Reference: Martine Bedin, Super lamp prototype, made by Fausto Celati for Memphis, 1981 (V&A, M.1-2011). The actual image was inspected: a blue semicircular body, black wheels and contrasting colored lamp sockets combine a domestic lamp with a toy-like form.

  • Mixed languages: editorial serif italics meet utilitarian monospace labels and a rounded sticker-like action.
  • Quotation and contradiction: a lamp-inspired arch and offset silhouette sit behind the tee; a formal headline meets an informal streetwear label.
  • Layering: tilted product and label planes suggest a personal outfit collage while keeping the copy horizontal and readable.

Intended effect: invite visitors to remix a familiar basic in their own way. Risk: the mixed signals may distract a visitor who wants simple styling guidance.
