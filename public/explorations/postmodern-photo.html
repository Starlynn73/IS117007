Why this revision?

The learner kept B because its fonts elevate the website, but found the clothing graphics too cartoony. This revision retains the typography and uses an AI-generated worn outfit to explore cool, effortless, gender-neutral chic. The original controlled A/B comparison remains available.

Reference: Martine Bedin, Super lamp prototype, made by Fausto Celati for Memphis, 1981 (V&A, M.1-2011). The actual image was inspected: a blue semicircular body, black wheels and contrasting colored lamp sockets combine a domestic lamp with a toy-like form.

  • Mixed languages: editorial serif italics meet utilitarian monospace labels and a rounded sticker-like action.
  • Quotation and contradiction: the original lamp reference informed B's mixed visual language. This revision trades its toy-like arch for an editorial photograph, retaining the contrast of a formal headline and informal streetwear label.
  • Layering: a lightly tilted outline and label frame an upright photograph. The full outfit stays visible, including sneakers.

Intended effect: invite visitors to remix a familiar basic in their own way. Risk: a single model cannot demonstrate how the garment would fit every visitor; this generated styling image is illustrative.
