00 / Direction

Everyday, elevated.

The chosen direction uses editorial typography and photographic outfit inspiration.

Audience hypothesis

College students meeting friends who want a confident go-to look, but worry a plain tee feels basic. A class-day styling need supplies a secondary check.

Everyperson

An approachable brand character: friendly, relaxed and encouraging. This describes the brand, not a personality assigned to buyers.

Postmodern remix

Serif italics, utility labels and a rounded lime action mix editorial and informal visual languages. Lightly tilted framing adds movement without hiding the outfit.

Audience needs are unvalidated hypotheses. The two original controlled hero explorations are preserved: modernist A and postmodern B. B’s photographic revision developed the chosen direction.

04 / Imagery

Show the whole idea.

Photographic outfit concepts with Black men and women. Keep styling open to all genders.

These example images are AI-generated outfit inspiration, not photographs of a tested product. Keep the tee white and unbranded; show useful accessory details. Images do not establish fabric, comfort, fit or manufacturing claims. Styling pieces are not offered for sale. The six-look storefront gallery extends this same visual treatment to smart casual, sporty casual, easy layers and relaxed weekend ideas.

05 / Components

A useful next step.

The same button and styling-tip classes appear on the storefront.

Find your outfit ↗Try the white tee ↗

Try this: echo a sneaker color in an accessory. Use what you own; nothing needs to match perfectly.

Reciprocity

Free styling ideas and accessory pairings offer practical help without a sign-up or purchase.

Consistency

An optional “Save … for my plans” choice opens that look’s checklist and a link to try the tee. The visitor chooses a look first; saving never adds to the bag. Plans clear on refresh and can be removed.

These are proposed persuasion mechanisms with a visitor benefit, not measured conversion improvements. Required size selection is validation, not the consistency application.

06 / Voice

Friendly.
Relaxed.
Encouraging.

Invite experimentation without suggesting someone’s style is inadequate.

Encourage ownership

“Use what you own; make it yours.”

Offer possibilities, with room to adapt or decline.

Be clear about the demo

“Choose a size before adding the shirt to your demo bag.”

Truthful feedback and fictional price labeling. No invented reviews, urgency, scarcity or unsupported product promises.
