What I noticed → what it changes.

Look, brand character & persuasion notes
  • UNIQLO: structured shopping controls with a collage-like campaign. Everyperson with Sage cues is a possible reading; ratings and bestseller messaging suggest social proof.
  • H&M: a functional shopping layout with expressive fashion photography. Everyperson with Lover cues is a possible reading; styled models may invite liking and reviews suggest social proof.
  • Everlane: restrained typography, whitespace and repeated image layouts. Sage with Caregiver cues is a possible reading; production information may invite authority-based trust.

These are interpretations of sampled pages, not official brand classifications. Claims and reviews were not independently verified. A grid alone does not establish Modernism, nor does a photograph establish Postmodernism. Full comparison · Framework sources.

04 / MY AUDIENCE CHOICE & REASON

Styling help for students meeting friends.

I chose position B and college students meeting friends. My earlier reason was to give people styling options they can copy or adapt: seeing the tee in a confident look may help them imagine recreating it.

Source → decision: all three retailers already show styled products. On the Go therefore needs a more specific task: help a student adapt a look for their plans, using pieces they may already own. The storefront now offers six outfit starting points, each with a pairing tip, a substitution and an optional checklist. More looks do not by themselves prove the guidance is useful.

Brand carried forward: Everyperson; friendly, relaxed and encouraging. Keep the On the Go wordmark and shared colors/type. Free outfit advice offers useful value before purchase (reciprocity); voluntarily saving a look opens a matching checklist (consistency). Buying stays optional and separate.

This summarizes existing choices; AI helped organize the evidence and proposed the expanded outfit content. No new explanation or customer preference is attributed to the learner. Detailed direction and brand notes →
