02 / TURN EVIDENCE INTO A CHOICE

Styling is the starting point.
Specific help is the experiment.

The existing On the Go direction is Everyperson: friendly, relaxed and encouraging. Keep the promise useful through complete looks, clear explanations and voluntary saved-look checklists. Do not turn desired confidence into an unsupported product claim.

Open question

What makes this guidance more useful than a competitor’s styled photo—or the tee already in the visitor’s drawer?
