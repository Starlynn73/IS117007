04 / RESEARCH LIMITS

What this evidence cannot establish.

  • Three selected retailers do not represent the whole market, market share or all college-student alternatives.
  • Different cuts and sizes prevent an equivalent-value or garment-quality comparison.
  • Product composition, fit, environmental claims, ratings and offers are retailer statements; no garment, supply-chain or review-authenticity audit was performed.
  • The original route began with product links. It does not measure how easily a first-time shopper would discover those items from the homepage.
  • Desktop screen captures do not establish mobile usability or full accessibility. The constrained UNIQLO views limit visible context.
  • No orders, contact-data entry, payment, delivery, returns execution, analytics or customer interviews were completed.
  • Personas, usefulness, confidence and purchase effects remain hypotheses. No peer Research feedback has yet been recorded in issue #3.
