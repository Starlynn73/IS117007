01 / Plans with friends

Streetwear, your way.

Relaxed jeans and statement sneakers give the white tee a direction. Add a slim chain or a simple watch; let one detail lead.

Try this: echo a sneaker color in an accessory. Use what you own; nothing needs to match perfectly.

Use what you own: Any jeans and your everyday sneakers work. The idea is the pairing, not a particular brand.

Your streetwear checklist

  • A white tee
  • Relaxed jeans
  • Your statement sneakers

02 / From class to coffee

Clean casual, easy.

Pair the tee with relaxed trousers and simple sneakers. A belt, small hoops or a shoulder bag can give familiar basics a finishing touch.

Try this: choose one accessory to repeat a color or shape. Keep the rest as simple as you like.

Use what you own: Use dark jeans if you do not have trousers. Try your usual tote instead of a shoulder bag.

Your clean casual checklist

  • A white tee
  • Relaxed trousers
  • Simple sneakers
  • A finishing detail: a belt, hoops or bag

03 / A presentation or dinner

A little more put together.

Let a white tee soften an open blazer and dark trousers. Simple loafers and a watch finish the look without adding much fuss.

Try this: leave the blazer open so the white tee gives the dark layers a clear contrast.

Use what you own: Try an open cardigan or an overshirt instead of a blazer, and clean sneakers instead of loafers. Check your event’s dress expectations.

Your smart casual checklist

  • A white tee
  • An open blazer, cardigan or overshirt
  • Dark trousers or neat dark jeans
  • Loafers or clean sneakers

04 / A laid-back campus day

Easy does it.

A plain tee, loose track pants and everyday sneakers make an easy off-duty combination. A tote or cap adds a useful finishing detail.

Try this: keep the tee simple and let the relaxed shape of the trousers set the mood.

Use what you own: Use joggers, drawstring trousers or leggings you already own. Choose your everyday trainers; this is styling inspiration, not a performance claim.

Your sporty casual checklist

  • A white tee
  • Track pants, joggers or leggings
  • Your everyday trainers
  • An optional tote or cap

05 / A cooler day out

One extra layer. New direction.

Wear an olive overshirt open over the tee, then add straight jeans and boots. The white layer breaks up darker pieces and keeps the outfit easy to read.

Try this: leave the outer shirt open and fold the cuffs once for a more relaxed finish.

Use what you own: A denim shirt, flannel or light jacket can replace the overshirt. Sneakers work if boots are not your thing.

Your easy layers checklist

  • A white tee
  • An open overshirt, denim shirt or light jacket
  • Straight jeans
  • Boots or sneakers

06 / A walk or weekend catch-up

Keep the weekend simple.

Tuck the tee loosely into sand-colored shorts and keep the shoes simple. A small crossbody bag gives you one less thing to carry.

Try this: try a loose front tuck to change the shape without needing a different tee.

Use what you own: Use shorts you own, a skirt or lightweight trousers. Any practical bag and familiar sneakers can carry the idea.

Your relaxed weekend checklist

  • A white tee
  • Shorts, a skirt or lightweight trousers
  • Simple sneakers
  • An optional crossbody bag or tote
